# 🏟️ Match Details

Every match becomes its own story — with score, timeline, statistics and AI analysis.

---

## Score & teams

<div align="center">
<img src="../screenshots/game-detail.png" width="320" />
</div>

- **Large score display** with color coding (Red = home, Blue = away)
- **Club names** with country flags (🇩🇪 Borussia Dortmund vs. 🇪🇸 Atletico Madrid)
- **Mode badge** (2v2)
- **Date** in long German format ("Thursday, 26 February 2026")
- **Team lineups** with player avatars or initials

---

## Match timeline (score timeline)

<div align="center">
<img src="../screenshots/spiele-details-3.png" width="320" />
</div>

The vertical timeline shows each goal chronologically — from bottom (kick-off) to top (final whistle):

- **Red dots & badges** — home goals (left)
- **Blue dots & badges** — away goals (right)
- **Center line** — time axis
- **Divider lines** with labels for extra time (n.V.) and penalties (n.E.)
- **Penalty icon** 🥅 for spot kicks

> Example: A 6:5 after penalties — with regular time, extra time and a dramatic penalty showdown.

## FC26 match statistics

<div align="center">
<img src="../screenshots/match-stats.png" width="320" />
</div>

If an FC26 screenshot was uploaded, all statistics are shown as an interactive comparison:

| Statistic | Display |
|-----------|---------|
| Possession | Percentage comparison with bars |
| Shots | Absolute values |
| Expected Goals (xG) | Expected goals (1 decimal) |
| Passes | Total count |
| Pass accuracy | Percentage |
| Duels | Absolute + won |
| Interceptions | Defensive actions |
| Saves | Goalkeeper performance |
| Fouls / Corners / Yellow cards | Other match events |
| Dribbles / Shot accuracy | Percentages |

Each stat has a **colored comparison bar** — the dominant team is highlighted.

## AI match report

<div align="center">
<img src="../screenshots/match-report.png" width="320" />
</div>

Generated **automatically** once match stats are available. The report is:

- **Written in German**
- **Entertaining** in tone — like a sports commentator
- **Data-driven** — includes possession, xG, career data
- **Narrative** — detects stories like underdog wins, comebacks, streak breaks

> *"What a crazy comeback by Borussia Dortmund! Atletico Madrid dominated for 80 minutes with 80% possession, but then the Dortmund duo struck back..."*

---

## Screenshot upload

FC26 screenshots are added on the match detail page — the wizard has no upload step. Right after saving, the app opens the new match, and the **FC26 screenshots** card sits below the score:

1. Tap **Add all three now**
2. Select the three FC26 stat screenshots: **Overview**, **Passes** and **Defence**
3. AI automatically extracts all statistics
4. Once all three are in, a match report is generated

> Recorded matches get their stats from the recording automatically — there the upload is only a fallback, folded away under **Upload statistics manually**.
